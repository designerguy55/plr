What is AI Image Guides Vault?

The AI Image Guides Vault is a collection of 30 short, focused guides, each one dedicated to a single popular AI image style, from thumbnails and product photos to book covers and social posts.

Anyone can open an AI image tool, but almost nobody knows what to type into it, and a thumbnail prompt doesn't work for a product photo any more than a book cover prompt works for a social carousel. Every style has its own language, its own keywords, and its own formula, and guessing your way there wastes hours and credits on generic, forgettable results.

Every guide in the Vault breaks down exactly how to structure a winning prompt for its style, gives you a ready-to-use formula, hands you 10 done-for-you prompts you can copy and paste, and flags the common mistakes that ruin the result. No guesswork, no wasted generations.
