What is Photo-to-Art Prompt Generator?

Photo-to-Art Prompt Generator is a prompt-engineering tool with 50 pre-engineered style presets for turning photos of people, pets, and groups into stylized portraits, from 3D movie characters and anime to oil paintings, claymation, and retro 1980s yearbook looks.

Turning a photo into AI art is still hit and miss. Type "make this a cartoon" and what comes back is often a stranger, with the wrong face and the wrong hair. Every style needs its own prompt language, and keeping the likeness while changing the look is the hard part for most people.

With this tool, you pick a subject type, click a style, and get a complete, detailed prompt with the style details and likeness-keeping rules already built in. Attach the photo in your AI image tool, paste the prompt, and you're done.

How It Works

Pick a subject, click a style, get a ready-to-use prompt out.
