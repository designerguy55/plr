Turning a photo into AI art is still hit and miss.

You upload a photo and type something like "make this a cartoon." What comes back is a stranger. The face has changed, the hair is wrong, and the style is only vaguely what you pictured. So you rewrite the prompt, try again, and get a different stranger.

It gets worse when the photo is of a pet, or a couple, or a whole family. One person gets restyled and the others get swapped, merged, or cropped out.

And most of the time, the problem isn't the AI tool. It's the prompt.

Every art style needs its own prompt language, and keeping the likeness while changing the look is the hard part for most people.

A claymation figure needs different wording than a Renaissance oil painting, a pixel-art sprite, or a 1970s film snapshot. Each style has its own lighting, color palette, proportions, and background. A good prompt also has to tell the AI what to change and what to keep: the face, the hair, the features, the pose. Writing all of that from scratch for every style you want to try takes far longer than it should. Now there's a tool built specifically to solve that.
