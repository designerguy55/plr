Asking an AI to "make my photo look amazing" gets you a filter, not a scene.

You upload a regular photo and type something like "make this epic." What comes back is the same photo with more contrast, a face that has quietly changed, and a background that still looks like your living room.

The images that stop people mid-scroll are built from specific decisions. Where is the subject? What is the weather doing? Where is the light coming from? What is flying through the air? What is the camera angle? Skip those details and the picture stays ordinary.

Most of the time, the problem isn't the AI tool. It's the prompt.

Describing a scene in enough detail, while keeping the face intact, is the hard part.

"Stormy" is not a scene. A great storm image needs the right sky, light, flying debris, lens, and framing. A great action shot needs an extreme angle, motion blur in the right places, and sharp detail on the subject. A good prompt also has to tell the AI what to keep, so the face, hair, and features stay put while the clothing, setting, and light change. Writing all of that from scratch every time is slow. What Should Change?

Restyle the outfit for the scene, or keep your own.

A parka looks right in a snowstorm and a riding jacket looks right on a motorcycle. Every scene comes with outfit guidance, and a simple selector tells the tool what to change.

Restyle My Outfit (Default)

The clothing is restyled to suit the scene, like a heavy parka for a blizzard or a fitted jersey for a diving catch, while the prompt tells the AI to keep the face and hair color unchanged.

Keep My Original Outfit

Change only the scene, lighting, and photographic look, and keep the clothing from your original photo.

Pets get matching accessories only when they suit the scene, and Text to Image mode can dress the subject for the scene or leave clothing out of the prompt.

Built To Keep Faces Readable

Big, wild scenes, with the subject still recognizable.

Extreme weather, flying debris, and fisheye angles can easily swallow a face. The prompts are written to prevent that.

Likeness lock

Every photo prompt tells the AI what to preserve, like face shape, hair color, skin tone, eye color, and distinctive features.

Debris stays clear of the face

Snow, sand, spray, and sparks fly through the foreground and background, and the prompt tells the AI to keep them off the face.

No helmets, masks, or goggles

Action scenes ask for the face to be fully visible, instead of hidden behind gear or heavy blur.

Photorealistic only

Every prompt blocks cartoon, anime, illustration, 3D render, and fantasy-character styling, so you get realistic photos.

+

No. The tool writes the prompts. You use them in any AI image tool that lets you upload a photo, including free versions of ChatGPT and Gemini, or paid tools. You will need your own access to one of them.

Will the result look exactly like me?

+

AI image tools vary, and no prompt can guarantee an identical likeness. Every photo prompt includes instructions to preserve the face, hair, and features, which helps the result stay recognizable. Wide scenes show more of the surroundings than your photo contains, which the AI will invent, and very busy scenes like storms and action shots can sometimes cover the face. The Refine section lets you tone things down or fix small things after the first result.

Does my photo get uploaded anywhere?

+

No. The tool never asks for your photo. It only writes the prompt. You attach your photo directly in your own AI image tool along with the prompt.

Can I use it without a photo?

+

Yes. Text to Image mode builds a brand-new scene from your description and your chosen style, so you can create images with no photo at all.

Will it change my clothing?

+

By default, yes. The "What Should Change?" selector restyles the outfit to suit the scene, and you can switch it to keep your original outfit instead.

Does it include anime, cartoon, or fantasy styles?
