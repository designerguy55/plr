Under The Hood

Every preset is built from the same six locked-in ingredients.

A style preset isn't just a name and a thumbnail. Click one, and the tool fills in six specific settings behind the scenes — the exact combination that makes that style look the way it does, every single time.

  • Art Style DefinitionThe core visual identity of the style — kawaii, mandala, gothic fantasy, and so on
  • Complexity LevelSimple, moderate, or intricate detail, matched to the style and its intended audience
  • Line Weight & CharacterExact stroke thickness and line quality, locked in so it never drifts from page to page
  • Mood & Age FitThe emotional tone of the style and who it's best suited for, from toddlers to adults
