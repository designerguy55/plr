Frequently asked questions

Do I need to pay for an expensive AI tool to use this?

No. The prompt generator works with free versions of ChatGPT, Gemini, or Midjourney, as well as paid image generation tools.

How does this save time over normal AI image prompts?

Instead of manually writing long descriptive prompts or fighting AI through trial-and-error, you type your headline and offer, pick a few dropdowns, and instantly get a fully engineered prompt with a clear text hierarchy, layout, sizing, and style already built in.

Will the AI always spell my ad text correctly?

The prompts include explicit rules that tell the AI to render your text exactly as written, use only the text you provide, and keep it legible. AI image tools can still make occasional mistakes, so it's always worth checking the result before you publish. Short headlines and offers render the most reliably.

Is this a recurring subscription fee?

No. You pay once for lifetime access to the generator tool. There are no monthly fees or hidden costs.

What platforms and sizes does this work for?

It includes prompts sized for Instagram and Facebook feed ads, Stories, Reels and TikTok, LinkedIn, display banners (300×250, 728×90, 160×600, 970×250), email headers, and website hero banners. You can also type in a custom size.

Can it create very wide or narrow banner sizes?

The prompt describes the exact size you choose. However, many image generators can't produce extreme shapes like a 728×90 strip natively. For those sizes, the tool reminds you to generate at the nearest standard ratio and then crop or extend the image to fit.

Can I add my own logo and brand colors?

You can enter your brand name and brand hex codes, and the prompt will use them. AI image tools can't reliably reproduce a real logo, so the best approach is to add your actual logo to the finished image afterward.

What format do I receive the product in?

You receive the standalone web tool in a single lightweight HTML file that opens instantly in any web browser on desktop or mobile.
